This appendix presents supporting materials from the partner consultation, prototype demonstration, and vehicle inspection. The materials support the methodology in Chapter~\ref{ch:method} and the implementation evidence in Chapter~\ref{ch:result_discuss}.

The research team coordinated with Hino Paranaque Subic GS Auto Inc. (SGSA) to inspect the sample fleet-vehicle environment, review the intended monitoring workflow, and demonstrate the prototype interfaces. The evidence photographs in Figures~\ref{fig:appendix_partner_initial_consultation}--\ref{fig:appendix_partner_additional_demo_two} document the consultation, fleet-vehicle inspection, dashboard review, and prototype demonstration activities.
